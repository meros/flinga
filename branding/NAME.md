# Name

Recommendation: **Flinga**. Runner-up: **Flimmer**. Both have a banner in `out/`.

Constraints: no "Telia" and nothing that imitates its branding (an independent client must not read as
the operator's product); 1–2 words, at most 12 characters; easy to say in Swedish and English; evokes
speed, lightness, live TV or zapping.

| Name | Rationale | Clash check (web search, 2026-10-15) |
|---|---|---|

and anything with "Play", "Lite" or "TV" in it (generic and close to the operator's own product names).

## Why Flinga

- It is the only candidate with no same-category clash in the searches above.
- It carries the product promise (light) without claiming a number, and pairs with an algorithmic mark:
  the Koch snowflake is the textbook fractal, and the play triangle sits in its centre.
- Six letters, no å/ä/ö, so it works as a launcher label, a package suffix and a file name without
  transliteration.

## What these checks are not

These were general web searches, not trademark searches. Before publishing the app (Play Store or
anywhere public), search EUIPO's eSearch and PRV's Svensk varumärkesdatabas for the chosen name in
classes 9 (software) and 38/41 (telecoms, entertainment).
